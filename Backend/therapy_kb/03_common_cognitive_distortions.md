# Common Cognitive Distortions & Reframing
**therapy_type**: cbt
**issue**: general

- All-or-nothing: “If not first rank, total failure.”
- Catastrophizing: “One bad mark = ruined future.”
- Mind reading: “My parents are disappointed in me.”
- Fortune telling: “I know I will fail.”

Reframing steps: Label the distortion → Examine evidence → Find balanced thought.
