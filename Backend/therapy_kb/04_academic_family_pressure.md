# Handling Academic & Family Pressure (India-Specific)
**therapy_type**: cbt
**issue**: academic_family

High expectations from competitive exams, joint families, and “log kya kahenge” are common.
CBT helps by:
- Recognising perfection is not required for love.
- Setting small, realistic goals.
- Kind boundary communication: “I am trying my best.”
Balanced view: Success matters, but mental health and steady effort also matter.
